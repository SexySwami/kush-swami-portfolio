OV-01THE BRAND

Gully Klassics is an independent Toronto streetwear label: outerwear, headwear and fits rooted in hip-hop culture.

OV-02THE INSIGHT

“It’s not just the clothes I wear; it tells my story.” Streetwear buyers dress to show who they are and where they’re from.

OV-03THE IDEA

Toronto By Night: the week is done, real life starts. Make Gully Klassics part of the city’s nightlife and music scene.

OV-04THE PLAN

Paid social, local hip-hop influencers, a blog, organic social, SEO, display and email, over 16 weeks for $13.5K.

03 — THE BIG IDEA

Toronto comes alive at night.

The work week is done, but real life is just starting. That’s the Toronto lifestyle. And what’s more Toronto than Gully Klassics? We position it as part of the city’s nightlife and music scene. No studio shoots or hard sell. Just the city, our friends, the lights, good music and Gully Klassics.

Streetwear
= Digital

Social media is the #1 influence for streetwear fans. The campaign lives on the feeds where they already find new drops.

Streetwear
= Music

80% of respondents say rap is a major influence on streetwear, so the city’s hip-hop artists are the brand’s natural voice.

Streetwear
= Identity

Its roots are in ’80s and ’90s rap icons, whose hustle and determination made them stars. Gully Klassics wears that story.

05 — INFLUENCER MARKETING

Worn by the
local scene.

Our audience follows and trusts local artists who share their values. So instead of renting celebrity reach, we put Gully Klassics on ten Toronto hip-hop musicians and creators and let them wear it their way.

@LOCAL.ARTIST · INSTAGRAM

“Rocking my favourite gear from Gully Klassics at tonight’s show. Big ups for supporting independent artists! Cop yours now! #hiphop #indieartist #fashion #toronto”

@LOCAL.ARTIST · TIKTOK

“Just got my hands on these fresh threads from Gully Klassics! Love how they support local artists like me. Check out my new look and get yours at gullyclassics.com. #supportlocal #hiphopfashion #ad”

Tactic 01 · Instagram giveaway

Follow, like, tag three.

A low-effort, cost-effective way to build awareness and grow the audience. To enter: follow, like, tag three friends and be based in the GTA.
