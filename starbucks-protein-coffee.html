Case study · Creative brand strategy

It’s Your Move.

Launching Starbucks Protein Coffee in Canada with a campaign that doesn’t shout at people to work out — it reminds them it’s okay to lack motivation, and hands them a reason to move.

00 — Overview

The campaign
in 30 seconds.

The product

A canned cold coffee with 10 g of collagen protein, electrolytes and 150 mg of caffeine — $4.99, in grocery stores Q3 2024.

The problem

4 in 10 Canadians are intimidated by the gym. The barrier isn’t energy — it’s motivation and fear of judgment.

The idea

A ‘Wall of Motivation’ at a downtown Toronto GoodLife gym, where strangers leave sticky-note encouragement for each other.

The goal

30% awareness among Canadians aged 20–34 and +50% social mentions by the end of Q3 2024.

01 — The problem

Starting is
the hardest rep.

Canadians want to be active — but walking into a gym can feel overwhelming. The research pointed to a motivation gap, not a fitness gap.

40%of Canadians are intimidated to work out in a gym.

38%of them avoid exercising in public because they feel watched and judged.

33%say they don’t know what they’re doing or where to start.

Consumer insight“I’m too tired and don’t feel inspired to get active. If only there was a drink that could give me the motivation to go work out.”
02 — The product

Coffee that
pulls its weight.

Starbucks Protein Coffee: a lightly sweetened, shelf-stable canned cold coffee built for active days.

10 gcollagen protein — fast-absorbing for recovery, and good for skin
150 mgcaffeine from coffee, under Health Canada’s 180 mg cap
3electrolytes — sodium, magnesium and potassium for hydration
$4.99per can, launching in grocery stores nationwide in Q3 2024

Why collagen? It absorbs quickly to support muscle recovery and is known for skin benefits — two things whey and most other protein sources can’t both claim — which broadens the drink beyond hardcore gym-goers.

04 — The competition

Win on lifestyle,
not ingredients.

The shelf was crowded with sports-nutrition brands competing on formulas. Starbucks had something they didn’t: brand love and a café on every corner.

BrandProductPrice (CAD)Positioning
BioSteelSports drink$2.99Premium, science-backed performance for athletes
VegaProtein shake$3.49Plant-based, clean-label nutrition
CelsiusFitness drink$2.99Thermogenic energy for workouts
Starbucks Protein CoffeeProtein cold coffee$4.99An everyday coffee that makes any active moment more rewarding

Product differentiation

Leading with protein, electrolytes and collagen turns the drink into a spec sheet — and “exercise performance coffee” doesn’t give people a picture to hold onto.

Lifestyle positioning

Show the drink inside an active, everyday life. It lets Starbucks start associating its brand with healthy living — reaching people who don’t yet see it as a healthy option.

Pricing at $4.99 sits $1–2 above competitors — what customers will pay for the Starbucks name — while matching the brand’s other canned drinks and staying well below the $7–8 ceiling for a single can.

06 — SWOT

Where Starbucks
stands.

SStrengths

  • High brand recognition and loyal customers
  • Known for innovation and quality coffee
  • Existing café and grocery distribution
  • Recyclable aluminium cans

WWeaknesses

  • Coffee lovers and fitness fans are sceptical audiences
  • Risk of confusion about ingredients and benefits
  • Brand dilution if seen as low quality
  • Premium price point

OOpportunities

  • New occasions: breakfast, pre/post-workout
  • Seasonal flavours that mirror the café menu
  • Competitors are often very high in sugar
  • Partnerships with gyms, brands and creators
  • ‘Proffee’ already familiar from social media

TThreats

  • Big energy-drink brands on the same shelf
  • Many substitutes — needs strong differentiation
  • Coffee supply-chain costs
  • Recession squeezing RTD spending
  • Changing caffeine regulation
07 — The big idea

A wall built
from encouragement.

Outside a high-traffic GoodLife Fitness in downtown Toronto — with a Starbucks nearby — we put up a Wall of Motivation. Passers-by, gym-goers and people deciding whether to go in leave sticky notes for each other. Together they make a rainbow of motivation.

you got this!
you’re not alone!
one day at a time!
unleash your potential
be unstoppable
embrace the energy
#ItsYourMove

The messages don’t stay on the wall. They’re printed on the cans, carried into GoodLife gyms across Canada, and become the copy for display and out-of-home ads — so the people who joined the campaign literally write it.

No product sampling at the event: the wall, notes and materials carry the Starbucks logo and hashtag, keeping it about encouragement, not a sales pitch.

Emotional

Help people get past the barriers to exercise and feel empowered on their own terms.

Social

#ItsYourMove invites people to share the small ways they stay active — and lift others up.

Inspirational

Real people reaching their goals, with Starbucks Protein Coffee as a companion, not a coach.

08 — Rollout

From one wall
to the whole country.

The event is recorded and becomes the engine for every channel.

  1. The event

    Wall of Motivation goes live at a downtown Toronto GoodLife Fitness.

  2. 60-second TV spot

    A pan-Canadian commercial cut from the real event footage.

  3. 30s & 15s social

    Cut-downs for social, ending on #ItsYourMove and a call to post your own message.

  4. OOH & display

    Real messages from the wall become billboard and banner copy.

  5. On the can & in gyms

    Phrases printed on packaging and placed in GoodLife locations nationwide.

09 — Goals

How we’d
measure it.

30%

Awareness

Raise awareness of Starbucks Protein Coffee to 30% of Canadians aged 20–34 by the end of Q3 2024, measured with surveys before and after the campaign.

  • S 20–34 Canadians
  • M pre/post surveys
  • T end of Q3 2024
+50%

Social mentions & search

Grow social mentions and search traffic by 50% by the end of Q3 2024, tracked with Google Trends and social listening around #ItsYourMove.

  • S mentions + search
  • M social listening
  • T end of Q3 2024

Creative guardrails

It’s okay to lack motivation.

No drill-sergeant energy — the spot is a refreshing reminder, not a lecture.

Positive affirmations only.

Stay well away from diet-centric messaging.

End on the hashtag.

Every piece closes on #ItsYourMove with a call to share your own message.

It’s your move.
